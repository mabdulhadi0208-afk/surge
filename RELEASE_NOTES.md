## Surge 3.0: the biggest update yet

**Download:** Mac (Apple silicon): **Surge-mac-arm64.dmg**, drag Surge to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open the first time. Windows: **Surge-win-x64.exe** (if SmartScreen asks: More info, then Run anyway). Already have Surge? It updates itself.

- **A whole new look, part of the Lumen family.** Every panel, menu, dialog and window now uses Lumen 4.0's design: its colours and type, the dark sidebar and big section banners in Settings, liquid glass, gem icons, and light and dark modes that follow your Mac.
- **Zero lag.** Surge now sleeps when you are not using it. Its windows truly close, nothing animates in the background, and it no longer checks things every second. Idle CPU is close to zero.
- **A new menu-bar icon in Surge colours.** The glossy Surge mark, a charge chip with the level and percent, or a battery that turns amber when low and cyan while charging, with versions made for light and dark menu bars that switch by themselves. While charging, the battery's fill flows like liquid; on battery it flows once when the level changes, then rests. Prefer plain? Turn on Monochrome icon in Settings, or turn off Animated battery.
- **A new right-click menu and new dialogs**, drawn by Surge in the same style instead of plain system boxes.
- **A beautiful update card.** See the download fill up, restart in one click, and get a short "what's new" card when Surge comes back.
- **Updates you can trust.** Surge checks GitHub every 30 minutes (and when you open its menu), retries calmly if the network drops, and never interrupts what you are doing.
- **New glassy sounds** for charging events and the interface, made by Surge itself. Off by default: turn them on in Settings.
- **Click Ripple, rebuilt**: each click draws in a tiny window that closes right after, instead of a full-screen layer that stayed open all day.
- **A new welcome tour and About page.**
